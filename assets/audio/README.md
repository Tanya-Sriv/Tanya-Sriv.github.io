# Mascot voice clips

Drop short audio files here and the mascot uses them instead of text-to-speech:

- banana.mp3           → "ba-na-naaa!"
- bello.mp3            → "bello!"
- beeboop.mp3          → "bee-boop!"
- laugh.mp3            → "hee-hee-ha!" (his laugh)
- banana-question.mp3  → "ba-naa-na?!"

Rules of thumb:
- Keep clips under ~2 seconds; mp3 or m4a both work (rename in main.js if m4a).
- Only use audio you own: record yourself or a kid you know (phone voice memo
  is fine), or audio you generated/licensed. Do NOT clip from movies or
  YouTube — that audio is copyrighted and can get the site taken down.
- Missing files are fine — those phrases just use the browser voice.

Fun tip: record a kid saying the phrases with energy, then keep the giggles
between takes. The imperfections ARE the charm.
